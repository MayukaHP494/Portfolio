01 / Engineering contribution

Building the components.

As an aerodynamics team member, I contributed to the practical work behind the car's aerodynamic components and carbon-fibre seat.

Moulds & carbon fibre

Contributed to mould finishing, carbon-fibre layups and component finishing for front- and rear-wing elements, side diffusers and side panels.

Contributed to manufacturing the carbon-fibre seat alongside the team's composite work.
